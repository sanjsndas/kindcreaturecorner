❓ Quick answers before you reach out

📅 How do I book an appointment?

You can call us directly, use the form above, or visit our online portal (link sent upon request). Same-day visits often available for urgent cases.

🚑 Do you offer emergency services?

Yes — we have a 24/7 triage phone line. For life-threatening emergencies, we coordinate with local 24h animal hospitals and can provide immediate stabilization.

🐕‍🦺 Do you accept new clients?

Absolutely! We welcome all species and breeds. New client registration can be done over the phone or via our contact form.

💰 Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we partner with care credit and offer in-house wellness plans for vaccinations, checkups and dental cleanings. Ask our team!
