Privacy Policy Overview

At Kind Creature Corner, your privacy and the security of your personal information are paramount. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect data when you visit our website, use our pet care services, or interact with our staff.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, billing address, pet’s medical history, insurance details, and payment data (processed via PCI-compliant gateways).
Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and appointment interactions through our booking system.
Pet Health Records: Vaccination history, lab results, diagnostic imaging, and treatment notes — all kept confidential in compliance with veterinary-client-patient privilege.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to provide veterinary care, process transactions, send appointment reminders, improve our services, communicate about promotions (with opt-out option), and comply with legal obligations. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Sharing & Disclosure

We may share information with: (a) veterinary specialists or laboratories to support your pet’s care; (b) third-party service providers (payment processors, cloud storage) under strict confidentiality agreements; (c) law enforcement when required by law or to protect safety. Any data sharing for marketing requires explicit consent.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard safeguards including encryption, access controls, and regular audits. Medical records are retained for a minimum of 7 years as per California veterinary board regulations. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting our Privacy Officer.

5. Your Rights (CCPA & GDPR)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what data we hold, request deletion, and opt-out of any sale of data (we do not sell). For EU residents, similar rights apply under GDPR. To exercise your rights, email privacy@kindcreaturecorner.com or call our office.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us.

7. Updates to Privacy Policy

We may revise this policy from time to time. Any material changes will be notified via email (if you have an active account) or by a notice on our website. The effective date is listed at the top of this hub.

🍪 How We Use Cookies & Similar Technologies

This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them on the Kind Creature Corner website, and your choices regarding cookie management. By continuing to browse our site, you consent to our use of cookies as described below, unless you adjust your browser settings.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, analyze site traffic, and improve your experience.

Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for website functionality, such as secure login, booking session management, and form submissions. These cannot be disabled without breaking core features.

Performance & Analytics Cookies: We use Google Analytics (anonymized IP) to understand how visitors interact with our pages, which helps us optimize content. These cookies do not identify you personally.

Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences (language, text size, accessibility settings) and provide personalized features like saved appointment drafts.

Marketing/Targeting Cookies: We may use limited advertising cookies (e.g., Facebook Pixel) to show relevant pet care content or retargeting campaigns. You can opt-out via your browser or third-party tools.

Third-Party Services

Our website may integrate embedded content (e.g., videos from YouTube, reservation widgets from Vetspire). These third-party services may place their own cookies. We do not control those cookies; please review their respective policies.

Managing Cookies

You can modify your cookie preferences at any time via your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Please note that blocking necessary cookies may affect booking and login features. For instructions on how to manage cookies on Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge, visit the “Help” section of your browser.

Additionally, you can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Consent & Changes

When you first visit our site, a cookie banner will request your consent for non-essential cookies. You may change your consent by clicking the “Cookie Preferences” link in the website footer. We will update this Cookies Policy as needed; changes will be posted here with a revised date.

📧 Cookie Inquiries: If you have questions about our cookie usage, please email webmaster@kindcreaturecorner.com. We value your digital privacy as much as your pet's comfort.
