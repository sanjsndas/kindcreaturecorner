🍪 Clear as a treat jar — Our Cookie & Tracking Policy

We use cookies to improve your experience and keep Kind Creature Corner’s website running smoothly, just like a happy, healthy pet.

📅 Effective: April 2, 2026 | Last update: April 2, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, analyze site traffic, and make our website function properly. Think of them as tiny digital helpers that enhance your visit — like a comfy bed for your pet’s data journey. 🐾

How Kind Creature Corner uses cookies

We use cookies for strictly necessary functions, performance improvement, and limited analytics to give you the best experience when booking appointments, reading pet wellness guides, or contacting our veterinary team. We never use cookies to track you across unrelated third-party services or for unsolicited advertising.

We do NOT use fingerprinting, cross-site tracking, or sell any cookie-related data. Our analytics respect "Do Not Track" signals when enabled.

Your control — Manage cookie preferences

You decide which non-essential cookies to allow. Essential cookies keep our veterinary booking system and security features intact and cannot be disabled without breaking core functionality.

🍪 Customize your cookie settings: Choose what you're comfortable with.

Manage specific cookie groups

You can also change your cookie settings any time by clicking the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies via settings — however, disabling essential cookies may impact appointment scheduling.

Third-party cookies and embedded content

Occasionally we embed videos from YouTube (e.g., pet care tutorials) or maps from Google Maps for directions to our Blue Harbor District clinic. These third parties may set their own cookies. We encourage you to review their privacy policies. We also use a secure payment widget (Stripe/PayPal) that may set functional cookies to process transactions — those are strictly necessary for checkout.

🐕 Our commitment: We never enable third-party advertising cookies without your clear consent. If you see any pet-related ads from us on other platforms, it's because you subscribed to our newsletter (and gave explicit permission), not because of random tracking.

Legal compliance: GDPR, CCPA, and ePrivacy

Kind Creature Corner complies with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and UK ePrivacy Directive. We provide transparent cookie disclosures and require opt-in consent before placing any non-essential cookies on your device. For California residents, we honor global privacy control (GPC) signals, automatically disabling non-essential cookies.

Your consent is stored for 6 months; you can withdraw it at any time using our preference center.

How to disable cookies in your browser

If you prefer to block all cookies, you can adjust your browser settings. However, note that certain website features (like appointment booking, contact forms, and login) may become unavailable or work incorrectly. Below are links to popular browser guides:
