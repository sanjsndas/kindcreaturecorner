🔒 Your Privacy, Our Promise

At Kind Creature Corner, protecting your personal data and your pet’s well-being go paw in paw. Read how we safeguard every interaction.

📅 Effective from March 15, 2026 | Last revised: April 2, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Kind Creature Corner (“KCC”, “we”, “us”, “our”) collects information to provide high-quality pet care, appointment scheduling, wellness plans, and customer support. The types of data include:

  • Personal identifiers – name, email address, phone number, postal address (for billing or service delivery).
  • Pet health details – medical history, vaccination records, dietary needs, and behavioral notes (shared only with veterinary staff).
  • Transaction data – services purchased, payment method (processed securely via PCI-compliant partners).
  • Communication preferences – SMS/email reminders, marketing consents, and feedback forms.
  • Website usage – cookies, IP addresses, and interaction analytics (see our Cookies Policy).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data exclusively to enhance your experience and ensure the best care for your companion. Purposes include:

  • 🐾 Scheduling and managing pet appointments, boarding, and grooming services.
  • 📧 Sending appointment reminders, health tips, and time-sensitive updates (e.g., vaccine due dates).
  • 💡 Improving our services, training our veterinary team, and handling emergency triage coordination.
  • 📊 Analyzing general trends to create better wellness resources.
  • ⚖️ Fulfilling legal obligations (e.g., medical records retention required by state veterinary boards).
🐕 Your trust matters: We never sell or rent personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Any data shared with labs or specialists is strictly for medical diagnosis with your consent.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your location, we process data based on:

  • Contractual necessity: To provide veterinary and pet care services you request.
  • Legitimate interests: Improving our operations, fraud prevention, and internal analytics.
  • Consent: For optional communications, newsletters, or certain pet wellness surveys.
  • Legal obligations: Record-keeping as required by California and federal veterinary regulations.

California residents have the right to know, delete, or opt-out of data “sales” (we do not sell data). For requests, email privacy@kindcreaturecorner.com.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We only share your information when necessary for the veterinary process or legal compliance:

  • ✅ Veterinary labs & pharmacies: To process tests or deliver medications directly.
  • ✅ Emergency referral hospitals: If your pet requires urgent specialized care beyond our facility.
  • ✅ Service providers: Secure payment processors (Stripe, PayPal), cloud storage (HIPAA-equivalent for vet data), and scheduling platforms. All sign strict confidentiality agreements.
  • ✅ Legal authorities: When required by law, court order, or to prevent harm.

We never share information with data brokers, advertisers, or non-related entities.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard safeguards: encrypted databases, role-based access, and regular security audits. Physical records are stored in locked facilities with restricted access. Retention periods:

  • 📂 Pet medical records: Minimum 7 years after last visit (or as required by CA veterinary law).
  • 📞 Contact inquiries: 24 months from last interaction.
  • 💳 Financial data: Retained only as long as needed for transaction disputes or accounting (up to 7 years).

In case of a data breach, affected users will be notified within 72 hours as required by law.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

✍️ Access & Correction

You may request a copy of your personal data or update inaccuracies by contacting our Privacy Officer at privacy@kindcreaturecorner.com or calling (415) 882-PETS.

🗑️ Right to Deletion

You can ask us to delete personal information not required for legal or medical retention. We will honor such requests unless an exception applies (e.g., active debt, ongoing treatment).

📢 Opt-out of Marketing

Every promotional email contains an unsubscribe link. You can also text "STOP" from your mobile device to end SMS reminders.

🛡️ Do Not Track Signals

Our website responds to global privacy control (GPC) signals as an opt-out of non-essential data sharing.

7. Children’s & Minor’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors. If a pet care profile is created by a guardian, all data is treated with care under parent/guardian consent.

8. International Data Transfers

Kind Creature Corner is based in the United States. If you access our website from outside the US, your data may be transferred to and processed in the US, where privacy laws may differ. By using our services, you consent to such transfer. For EU residents, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where applicable.

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this policy occasionally to reflect legal changes or service improvements. Material changes will be notified via email (if we have your address) or via a prominent notice on our website. The “effective date” at the top indicates the latest version.

🐾 Have questions or a privacy request?
Our dedicated Privacy Team is available Monday–Friday 9am–6pm PST. We aim to respond to verified requests within 15 business days.
